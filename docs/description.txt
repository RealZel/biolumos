Biolumos is a compact programmable light device designed around adjustable near-infrared output, portable operation, and straightforward session control. The hardware uses four 808-810 nm LEDs, each rated at 3 W electrical power with optical output up to 250 mW. The device layout was chosen to keep the form factor small while delivering consistent front-facing light output.

One of the defining features is flexible frequency control. Users can configure pulsing from 1 Hz to 10 kHz, and a continuous-wave mode is also available. This allows the same hardware to support a wide range of user-defined session styles instead of only a few built-in presets.

Another important design feature is the independent control of separate LED groups. In practice, one side of the array can run with one pulse pattern while the other side follows a different one, within the limits of the control logic. This provides more granularity than typical single-channel consumer devices.

The device also supports output adjustment from 1% to 100% and configurable session duration. A built-in vibration motor can be synchronized with the active light pattern, adding an optional tactile cue during use.

Portability and ease of use were key design goals. Biolumos can connect to a mobile phone via Bluetooth, to a computer or local network via Wi-Fi, or operate in standalone mode with one-button start. The integrated battery provides at least two full sessions on a single charge under typical operation. Firmware updates are supported over the air.

Ease of mounting was also considered in the physical design. The device uses a soft rubberized case together with a stretchable strap and hook-and-loop fastening. This mounting system helps secure the unit on many body areas while preserving comfort and freedom of movement.
