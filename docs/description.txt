The Biolumos device was designed after careful evaluation of LED wavelength, output power, and pulsing characteristics described in the photobiomodulation literature. After reviewing published data and comparing reported treatment windows, the development team selected a near-infrared range of 808-810 nm. This range is widely used in PBM research because it belongs to the optical window associated with comparatively deep tissue penetration. The final design uses four high-power LEDs of this spectrum. Each LED has an electrical power rating of 3 W and an optical output of up to 250 mW. Their placement in the housing was optimized to provide an irradiance of approximately 100 mW/cm2 at the treatment area, which corresponds to about 1 J/cm2 in 10 seconds of operation.

One of the device's defining features is flexible frequency control. Many commercial PBM systems operate only in continuous mode or offer a small set of fixed pulse options. Biolumos was designed differently: users can configure pulsing from 1 Hz to 10 kHz, and a continuous-wave mode is also available. This makes it possible to approximate a broad range of parameters reported in published PBM protocols rather than being limited to a few preset frequencies.

Another important advantage is the independent frequency generation for separate LED groups. In practice, this means one side of the LED array can operate at 10 Hz while the other side runs at 40 Hz, or any other combination supported by the control logic. This architecture gives researchers and clinicians a high degree of freedom for reproducing published protocols or exploring new protocol combinations.

The device also allows output power adjustment from 1% to 100% and supports configurable session duration. Together, these controls make the system suitable for highly individualized exposure settings. In addition, a built-in vibration motor can be synchronized with the light pulsing pattern, adding a tactile component to the treatment routine.

Portability and ease of use were key design goals. Biolumos is a compact autonomous device that can be carried in a pocket and used in different environments. It can connect to a mobile phone via Bluetooth, to a computer via Wi-Fi, or operate in standalone mode with a one-button start. The integrated battery provides at least two full sessions on a single charge for one user under typical operation. Firmware updates are supported over the air.

Ease of mounting was also considered in the physical design. The device uses a soft rubberized case together with a stretchable strap and hook-and-loop fastening. This mounting system helps secure the unit on many body areas while preserving comfort and freedom of movement.
