Biolumos is intended to be used as a consumer programmable light device, so practical handling and general light-safety precautions are important.

Light exposure
Avoid directing the device toward the eyes. Start with shorter sessions and lower output settings if you are unfamiliar with the hardware. If the device or the contact area feels unusually warm, pause the session and let the unit cool before using it again.

Device handling
Inspect the housing, strap, and charging cable regularly. Do not use the device if the enclosure is cracked, if moisture has entered the unit, or if the USB-C connector is damaged. Charge the device only with suitable power sources and keep it away from water during operation and charging.

Comfort and supervision
Stop use if you notice irritation, unusual heat, or discomfort. Children should use the device only with adult supervision. Store the device out of reach when not in use.

Operational interpretation
Session comfort depends on output level, session duration, placement, and contact conditions. For that reason, it is best to begin with conservative settings and adjust gradually based on user preference and normal operating comfort.
