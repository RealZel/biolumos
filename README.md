# Biolumos — Universal Portable Photobiomodulation Platform

Biolumos is a compact and accessible photobiomodulation (PBM) device designed for flexible near-infrared light protocols with adjustable pulsing, wireless control, and standalone operation. The platform combines broad protocol flexibility, true portability, and a body-mounting system for practical everyday use — making PBM technology available to everyone.

### Device Overview

Biolumos is built around four high-power 808–810 nm LEDs — a wavelength range at the core of modern PBM research, chosen for its favorable tissue penetration within the near-infrared optical window [1][2]. Unlike many consumer devices with fixed settings, Biolumos gives users full control: adjustable power output, session duration, and pulse frequency from 1 Hz to 10 kHz, along with continuous-wave mode.

The architecture supports independent control of separate LED groups, enabling complex multi-frequency protocols where different parts of the array operate at different settings simultaneously. A built-in vibration motor can synchronize with the pulsing pattern as an additional tactile indicator during sessions.

Biolumos is a universal and accessible PBM platform for wellness, sports recovery, rehabilitation support, and research applications.

### Key Capabilities

- Four high-power LEDs at 808–810 nm
- Adjustable output power from 1 % to 100 %
- Configurable pulse frequency from 1 Hz to 10 kHz
- Continuous-wave mode
- Independent channel control for different LED groups
- Session timer with repeatable protocol settings
- Wireless control via Bluetooth and Wi-Fi
- Standalone one-button operation — no phone required
- Built-in Li-ion battery — at least two full sessions per charge
- OTA firmware update support

### Mounting System

For convenient localized use, Biolumos fits into a soft TPU (thermoplastic polyurethane) case with a stretchable strap and hook-and-loop fastening. This system secures the device comfortably on virtually any body area while maintaining mobility during a session.

### How Photobiomodulation Works

Photobiomodulation (PBM) uses near-infrared light to stimulate natural processes inside cells. When 808–810 nm light penetrates tissue, it reaches the mitochondria — the organelles that produce energy for every cell in the body. The primary photoacceptor is **cytochrome c oxidase**, a key enzyme in the mitochondrial electron transport chain [1][2].

This interaction triggers a cascade of beneficial responses:

**Energy production.** Light absorption enhances the synthesis of adenosine triphosphate (ATP) — the universal energy molecule that fuels cellular repair, growth, and resilience. Cells with higher ATP levels recover faster and function more efficiently [1][2].

**Improved circulation.** PBM promotes the release of nitric oxide (NO), which relaxes blood vessel walls and improves local blood flow. Enhanced circulation delivers more oxygen and nutrients to the target area, supporting natural recovery [2][3].

**Inflammation control.** Research demonstrates that PBM modulates inflammatory signaling, reduces oxidative stress, and helps shift tissue from a pro-inflammatory state toward repair. This mechanism is particularly relevant for conditions involving chronic or excessive inflammation [3].

**Cellular repair activation.** Controlled low-level reactive oxygen species (ROS) generated during PBM activate transcription factors such as NF-κB and AP-1, switching on genes responsible for cell survival, proliferation, and tissue restoration [1][2].

These combined mechanisms make photobiomodulation one of the most promising non-invasive biostimulation technologies of the current decade. The evidence base continues to grow across diverse domains — from sports performance and post-exercise recovery [5] to musculoskeletal rehabilitation [6][7], neurological well-being [4], and post-stroke recovery support [8].

### Safety Notes

- Consult a licensed physician before starting any PBM routine.
- Do not direct the light into the eyes.
- Do not use the device over areas of suspected malignancy without explicit physician approval.
- Exercise additional caution during pregnancy, with children, and in cases of photosensitivity, fever, acute infection, or unstable medical conditions.
- Discontinue the session if pain, skin irritation, dizziness, or unusual symptoms increase.

According to published research, PBM at irradiance levels below 100 mW/cm² is considered safe, as the resulting temperature increase does not exceed 0.38 °C on the scalp and 0.06 °C in the brain [1][4].
